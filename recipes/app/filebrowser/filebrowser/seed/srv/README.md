# Northstar Relay

A small software company that automates large operational data exports.

## What is in here

- **Customers** — one folder per account, with its current invoice and the
  notes behind any open case.
- **Exports** — what the product actually did, run by run.
- **Finance** — twelve months of invoices, supplier bills and the ledger.
- **Product** — Release 2.8, its checklist and its open issues.
- **People** — the team, and the week ahead.

Everything here is demonstration data.
